How we use the standards

For each released course, these College Board materials establish the rules that matter to this practice product: multiple-choice question count and timing; unit/category or skill/practice weighting; topic and skill taxonomy; stimulus/set structure; calculator and timed-part rules; digital-delivery details that affect practice; and course-specific Section I source/task expectations.

Project engineering choices are documented separately and are not presented as College Board requirements. College Board is the authority if any discrepancy exists.
