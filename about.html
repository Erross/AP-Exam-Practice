AP EXAM PRACTICE

About AP Exam Practice

Free, unofficial, full-length AP multiple-choice practice using original questions and current course-specific exam configurations. No account required.

What you can practice

The current catalog has 29 released AP courses. For each one, the site builds a timed multiple-choice section using the course's configured question count, timing, weighting, skills or practices, stimulus sets, calculator rules, and timed parts where applicable.

  • Review the format before the timer starts.
  • Navigate, answer, change answers, cross out choices, and flag questions for review.
  • Refresh the same tab without losing an in-progress attempt while that browser session remains available.
  • See a practice score, per-unit breakdown, answer review, and explanations after submission.

Current scope

This is a multiple-choice practice site. It does not currently provide or score essays, FRQs, DBQs, SAQs, written responses, oral responses, portfolios, projects, or other performance tasks.

Eight audio-dependent AP courses are outside the current scope because they require listening, speaking, or aural workflows the site does not currently deliver: AP Music Theory and the non-Latin world-language/literature courses shown in the catalog.

A course card may show the official full-exam duration for context. The timer in this site applies to the multiple-choice practice section described on the card and preflight screen.

Original and unofficial

Questions are original practice material. They are not official College Board questions, secure AP material, or predictions of future exam questions. AP course names and exam structures are referenced only to describe the practice target.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or reviewed by College Board. Practice results are not official AP scores or score predictions.

Privacy and saved progress

No account is required. In-progress practice state is stored locally for the current browser tab/session rather than in a user profile or application database. Refreshing the tab can restore the attempt, but choosing Exit clears it and the site does not provide cross-device or long-term cloud saving.

How to use it

Choose an available course, review the preflight instructions and format, and start the timed section when ready. Use the results and explanations to identify weak areas, then take a different practice draw later.
